# Insights

## Executive summary
The dataset is clean, complete, and analysis-ready. There are 9,994 rows and 21 columns, with no missing values and no duplicate records. The business generated $2,297,200.86 in sales and $286,397.02 in profit, which produced a 12.47% profit margin.

## Key findings
1. Growth is strongest in the Consumer segment: Consumer sales totaled $1,161,401.35, ahead of Corporate and Home Office.
2. Technology is the highest-performing category: Technology delivered $836,154.03 in sales and $145,454.95 in profit.
3. Furniture is a profitability risk: Furniture generated $741,999.80 in sales but only $18,451.27 in profit, which is far below the other categories.
4. The biggest loss-making sub-categories are Tables (-$17,725.48), Bookcases (-$3,472.56), and Supplies (-$1,189.10).
5. West is the strongest region by sales and profit: $725,457.82 in sales and $108,418.45 in profit.
6. The top sales and profit product is the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier, which indicates that a small number of high-value office technology products are driving a large share of performance.
7. Shipping operations are stable: average shipping time is 3.96 days, and there are no order-date-to-ship-date anomalies.

## Business interpretation
The dataset suggests that the company is performing well overall, but profitability is concentrated in a smaller set of categories and products. The Technology category is an obvious growth driver, while Furniture is an area for operational review because several sub-categories are loss-making. The business should focus on protecting high-margin product lines while reviewing markdowns, product mix, and category-level profitability in Furniture.

## Recommendation
The next step is to build the Power BI dashboard using these validated KPIs and findings, then use interactive filters to explain category, region, and segment performance to stakeholders.
