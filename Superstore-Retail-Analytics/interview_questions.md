# Interview questions to practice

## Excel questions
- How did you identify duplicate records?
- Why are duplicate Order IDs not necessarily duplicates?
- How did you handle missing values?
- How did you validate date fields?
- What was the most important KPI you calculated?
- Why did you use PivotTables?

## SQL questions
- Explain your project SQL analysis.
- What is a subquery?
- What is CASE WHEN?
- What is a CTE?
- When would you use a CTE instead of a subquery?
- What is RANK?
- How is RANK different from ROW_NUMBER?
- What does PARTITION BY do?
- What is LAG used for?
- What is a running total?

## Python questions
- Why did you choose Pandas for this project?
- What did df.info() tell you?
- How did you check for missing values?
- How did you check duplicates?
- What did EDA reveal in this dataset?

## Power BI questions
- Why did you choose these KPIs?
- Why did you select these visuals?
- Which business question does each page answer?
- Why did you choose those slicers?
- What would you present to a manager?

## Project questions
- Why did you choose this dataset?
- What was the biggest data-quality issue?
- What was the most important insight you found?
- What would you improve with more data?
- What are the limitations of this analysis?

Answer these questions yourself first. Only then review the quality of your response.
