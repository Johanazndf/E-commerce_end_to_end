# Power BI dashboard plan

This section is for your dashboard work after the Excel, SQL, and Python analysis.

## Dashboard page 1: Executive sales overview
### Recommended KPIs
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity
- Profit Margin %

### Recommended visuals
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Segment
- Top 10 Products

### Recommended slicers
- Year
- Region
- Category
- Segment

### Business questions answered
- How is the business performing overall?
- Which category is contributing the strongest sales and profit?
- Which region has the highest performance?
- Which customer segment drives the most revenue?

## Dashboard page 2: Customer and product performance
### Recommended visuals
- Top Customers
- Top Products
- Bottom Products by Profit
- Sub-Category Performance
- Discount vs Profit
- Shipping Performance

### Recommended slicers
- Year
- Category
- Region
- Segment

### Business questions answered
- Which customers contribute the most sales?
- Which products are leading revenue or profit?
- Which products are underperforming?
- Are discounts linked to lower profitability?
- Is shipping performance stable?

## Important rule
Do not build a dashboard before the analysis is complete. The visuals should follow actual verified findings, not assumptions.
